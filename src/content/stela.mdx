## Overview

STELA is a ticketing and service escalation system developed for the House of Representatives of the Republic of Indonesia (DPR RI). The application enables users to submit service requests, track ticket progress, and review ticket histories through web and mobile platforms. It supports a structured workflow between the Service Desk and responsible officers to improve service coordination and transparency.

## Problem / Background

Managing service requests requires clear communication, traceable updates, and coordination between the Service Desk and the officers responsible for resolving issues. Without a centralized ticketing system, tracking request progress, documenting actions, and escalating unresolved issues can become difficult. A structured solution was needed to manage tickets throughout their lifecycle and provide visibility into their status. The application was developed as part of the final project for the MSIB program at DPR RI.

## Solution

Developed backend services to support ticket submission, status tracking, service escalation, and ticket history across web and mobile applications. Each submitted request receives a unique ticket code, allowing users to monitor its progress. The Service Desk manages incoming tickets, provides feedback, and escalates issues to the appropriate helpdesk or IT specialists. Officers report their actions through the application, and the Service Desk verifies updates to maintain a consistent ticket status record.

## Challenges

One of the main challenges was maintaining a consistent ticket lifecycle across multiple roles and stages of service handling. Ticket submissions, escalations, officer action reports, and Service Desk verification needed to follow a clear workflow so that status updates remained accurate and traceable. Supporting different user roles also required appropriate access control to ensure that users could perform only the actions permitted by their responsibilities.

## Result / Impact

STELA provides a centralized system for managing service requests and escalation processes within DPR RI. By organizing ticket submission, status tracking, officer updates, and Service Desk verification in one workflow, the application improves visibility into ticket progress and supports more structured coordination between service teams. The project also provided practical experience in developing backend services for a multi-role application with traceable workflows across web and mobile platforms.
