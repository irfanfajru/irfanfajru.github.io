## Overview

Daftrix is a web-based event management system designed to organize and manage pencak silat tournaments. It provides centralized tools for handling tournament operations, including registrations, brackets, categories, payments, teams, and match administration.

## Problem / Background

Managing pencak silat tournaments involves multiple interconnected processes, from participant registration and payment tracking to match scheduling and bracket organization. Handling these processes separately can make tournament administration more complex and increase the risk of inconsistent data and operational errors.

## Solution

Developed web-based event management system to centralized tournament management, covering registration, payment processing, bracket management, arena allocation, and match administration. Integrated third-party services, including Challonge, Google Drive, and Digiscore, to support tournament workflows, file storage, and scoring operations.
