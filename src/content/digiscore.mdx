## Overview

Digiscore is a digital scoring system designed for Pencak Silat tournaments. It enables judges to record and calculate scores in real time using accessible digital devices, improving scoring efficiency, accuracy, and transparency during competitions.

## Problem / Background

Traditional scoring processes in Pencak Silat tournaments can be time-consuming and challenging to coordinate, particularly when managing arenas, matches, and scoring. Manual scoring and communication can also increase the risk of delays and inconsistent information, making real-time score updates difficult.

## Solution

Developed a web-based digital scoring system that supports real-time scoring, match management, timer control, and violation verification. Integrated Digiscore with Daftrix to connect scoring operations with tournament administration, enabling more streamlined data exchange and tournament management.
